# Electromax — Executive Sales Performance Analysis

## Overview

This analysis evaluates Electromax's sales performance using an executive-level Power BI dashboard. The analysis focuses on **revenue, transaction volume, profitability, and product-category contribution**, then translates the findings into practical business actions.

The objective is not only to report performance, but to identify where management attention should be directed while keeping conclusions strictly aligned with the available data.

![Electromax Executive Overview](images/executive-overview-2025.png)

---

## 1. Revenue Performance Declined Year over Year

### Finding

In 2025, Electromax generated approximately **Rp95.40 billion in total revenue**, representing a **2.92% decline compared with the previous year**.

At the same time, total transactions declined by only **0.74%**, from 18,960 to 18,820 transactions.

| KPI | 2025 | Previous Year | YoY |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Total Revenue | Rp95.40 B | Rp98.27 B | **-2.92%** |
| Total Transactions | 18,820 | 18,960 | **-0.74%** |

### Analysis

Revenue declined more significantly than transaction volume.

Based on the available figures, the **Average Transaction Value (ATV)** also decreased:

- 2025: approximately **Rp5.07 million/transaction**
- Previous year: approximately **Rp5.18 million/transaction**
- Change: approximately **-2.20%**

This indicates that the decline in revenue was accompanied by a reduction in the average value generated per transaction.

### Business Implication

The current challenge is not simply increasing the number of transactions. The business also needs to improve the **value generated from each transaction**.

### Recommended Action

Prioritize initiatives that can increase transaction value, such as:

- Upselling customers to higher-value products.
- Bundling complementary products.
- Cross-selling relevant accessories or supporting products.
- Designing promotions around minimum transaction values rather than applying broad discounts.

The objective is to increase revenue **without relying solely on increasing transaction volume**.

---

## 2. Gross Profit Declined Alongside Revenue

### Finding

Gross Profit in 2025 reached approximately **Rp29.47 billion**, declining **3.15% YoY**.

| KPI | 2025 | Previous Year | YoY |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Gross Profit | Rp29.47 B | Rp30.43 B | **-3.15%** |

### Analysis

Gross Profit declined slightly faster than total revenue.

This means that the decline in sales performance was also reflected in the absolute amount of gross profit generated.

However, the available dashboard does not contain enough information to attribute this change to a specific cause such as discounting, product mix, pricing, or procurement cost. Therefore, no single cause is assumed in this analysis.

### Business Implication

Revenue recovery should not be pursued at the expense of profitability.

A strategy that increases sales but significantly reduces margin could improve revenue while weakening the quality of the business result.

### Recommended Action

Management should evaluate revenue recovery together with gross profit performance.

Recommended focus areas:

- Monitor gross profit contribution by product category.
- Compare revenue growth with gross profit growth.
- Evaluate the profitability impact of promotional activities.
- Prioritize sales growth that maintains a healthy margin.

---

## 3. Gross Profit Margin Remained Relatively Stable

### Finding

Gross Profit Margin (GPM) in 2025 was **30.89%**, compared with **30.96%** in the previous year.

The change was only approximately **-0.07 percentage point**.

| KPI | 2025 | Previous Year | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| GPM | 30.89% | 30.96% | **-0.07 pp** |

### Analysis

Although both revenue and gross profit declined, the gross profit margin remained relatively stable.

This suggests that the business was able to maintain a broadly similar gross margin level despite the decline in revenue.

### Business Implication

The current priority should be to **restore revenue while preserving margin quality**, rather than pursuing revenue growth through indiscriminate discounting.

### Recommended Action

Use GPM as a guardrail when designing sales and promotional strategies.

Recommended approach:

> **Grow Revenue + Maintain Healthy GPM**

Promotional programs should therefore be evaluated not only by additional sales generated, but also by their effect on gross profit.

---

## 4. Revenue Is Highly Concentrated in Television and AC

### Finding

Television and AC are the two largest revenue-generating subcategories in the dashboard.

- Television: approximately **Rp30 billion**
- AC: approximately **Rp23 billion**
- Combined: approximately **Rp53 billion**

Together, these two categories contribute approximately **55.6% of total revenue**.

![Revenue by Subcategory](images/revenue-by-subcategory.png)

### Analysis

Television and AC are clearly the dominant contributors to total revenue.

This makes them strategically important categories for maintaining overall sales performance.

At the same time, the remaining categories contribute a substantial portion of revenue and provide opportunities for additional growth.

### Business Implication

The business should maintain strong execution in its largest revenue categories while developing additional contributors rather than relying exclusively on the current revenue leaders.

### Recommended Action

#### Protect Core Categories

For Television and AC, maintain focus on:

- Product availability.
- Competitive pricing.
- High-demand product assortment.
- Targeted promotional campaigns.
- Cross-selling and bundling opportunities.

#### Develop Secondary Categories

Monitor categories such as:

- Kulkas
- Mesin Cuci
- Microwave
- Water Heater
- Vacuum Cleaner
- Small Appliance

The objective is not to reduce the importance of Television and AC, but to create additional sources of sustainable revenue growth.

# Business Recommendations

Based on the findings above, the recommended priorities are:

## Priority 1 — Increase Transaction Value

Revenue declined by 2.92% while transactions declined by only 0.74%, with Average Transaction Value also decreasing by approximately 2.20%.

**Action:**

> Strengthen upselling, bundling, and cross-selling strategies to increase value per transaction.

---

## Priority 2 — Recover Revenue Without Sacrificing Margin

Gross Profit declined by 3.15%, while GPM remained relatively stable at 30.89%.

**Action:**

> Pursue revenue recovery while maintaining GPM as a key profitability constraint.

---

## Priority 3 — Protect Core Revenue Drivers

Television and AC contribute approximately 55.6% of total revenue.

**Action:**

> Maintain strong inventory, pricing, assortment, and promotional execution for the two leading categories.

---

## Priority 4 — Build Additional Revenue Contributors

The remaining product categories represent opportunities to reduce reliance on the current dominant revenue sources.

**Action:**

> Identify secondary categories with attractive revenue, growth, and margin characteristics and develop them through targeted commercial strategies.

---

# Executive Takeaway

The 2025 performance indicates a **moderate deterioration in sales performance rather than a significant deterioration in margin quality**.

The main signals are:

1. **Revenue declined 2.92% YoY.**
2. **Transactions declined only 0.74%.**
3. **Average Transaction Value declined approximately 2.20%.**
4. **Gross Profit declined 3.15%.**
5. **GPM remained relatively stable at 30.89%.**
6. **Television and AC contributed approximately 55.6% of total revenue.**

The recommended business direction is therefore:

> **Increase transaction value, recover revenue, protect gross margin, and strengthen the product portfolio beyond the two dominant categories.**

---

# Analytical Boundaries

This analysis intentionally avoids attributing performance changes to specific causes that are not directly observable in the current dashboard.

For example, the analysis does **not** claim that revenue declined because of:

- Higher discounts.
- Lower product prices.
- Stock-outs.
- Changes in customer behavior.
- Changes in product mix.
- Changes in marketing activity.

Those explanations would require additional data and dedicated analysis.

The conclusions presented here are therefore limited to what can be supported by the available **revenue, transaction, profitability, and product-category data**.

This approach keeps the analysis evidence-based and avoids overclaiming beyond the data.

## Conclusion

The dashboard shows that Electromax's primary challenge in 2025 is **revenue performance**, while gross margin remains relatively resilient.

The most actionable opportunity is to improve the value generated from existing transactions, protect profitability during revenue recovery, and strengthen secondary product categories alongside the dominant Television and AC segments.

This transforms the dashboard from a reporting tool into a decision-support view:

> **What happened → What does the data indicate → What should the business prioritize?**
